Privacy Policy

This page explains how RentEZ uses information provided through the platform to operate its rental marketplace and related services.

Effective Date: 30 September 2026 RentEZ

1. Information We Collect

RentEZ may collect information required to provide account, rental, payment and platform services.

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • Rental orders, booking details and selected dates
  • Business information provided by rental businesses
  • Information required for payment and transaction processing

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and improve the RentEZ platform, including:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts
  • Processing rental orders and bookings
  • Checking equipment availability
  • Processing and verifying payments
  • Communicating booking and account updates
  • Supporting businesses and customers
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing misuse

3. Payment Information

Online payments are processed through our payment provider. RentEZ uses payment information only as necessary to complete and verify transactions and maintain payment-related records.

4. Business Information

Rental businesses may provide business registration, contact and verification information. Certain approved business information is displayed publicly so customers can identify the rental provider.

5. Communications

RentEZ may send account, booking, payment, cancellation, verification and other service-related notifications through available communication channels.

6. Data Security

RentEZ takes reasonable measures to protect information stored and processed through the platform. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

Users may review or update available account information through the platform. Questions regarding personal information can be directed to the RentEZ support team.
