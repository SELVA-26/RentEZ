Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about RentEZ accounts, rentals, bookings, payments and businesses.

How does RentEZ work?
Customers browse equipment or rental packages, choose their rental dates, add the required items to the cart, complete checkout and make payment securely through the platform.
Are businesses verified?
Yes. Businesses are reviewed by the RentEZ administrator before they can operate as an approved rental business on the marketplace.
How is equipment availability checked?
Availability is calculated using the selected rental dates and existing bookings. Package rentals also account for the physical equipment required by the package.
Can I rent multiple pieces of equipment?
Yes. Customers can add multiple equipment items and rental packages to the same order, subject to availability for the selected rental period.
How do rental packages work?
A package groups multiple pieces of equipment into a single rental offering. The business defines which equipment and quantities are included.
What payment method does RentEZ use?
RentEZ uses Razorpay for online payments. Payment status is recorded against the rental order after successful verification.
Can I cancel a rental?
Cancellation is available when the booking is eligible under the platform's cancellation rules. Refund handling depends on the payment and booking state.
What happens after payment?
After successful payment verification, the order and associated bookings are confirmed and the business can manage the rental from its dashboard.
How can businesses manage their inventory?
Approved businesses have a dedicated business portal where they can add equipment, update details, manage availability, create packages and manage bookings.
Who can I contact for support?
You can contact the RentEZ support team through the Contact page for account, booking, payment or platform-related assistance.
